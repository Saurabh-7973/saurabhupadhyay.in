QSO Ready: ham radio exam prep and Morse code

Practice tests for the Technician, General and Amateur Extra exams, answers that show the math and the rule, and a Koch Morse code course. It works offline, with no account and no ads.

What's inside

  • The real question pools, word for word. Every question, answer choice and answer key from the current pools, exactly as published and in the published order (answer shuffle is optional). Flag questions, review the ones you missed, and report an error on any question.
  • Answers that show their working. Every math question comes with a worked calculation. Every rules question quotes the rule with its Part 97 section and the date it was checked. Pool diagrams zoom with a pinch.
  • Practice exams built like the real one. One question from each topic group, with the real question count and pass mark. Untimed by default, with an optional timer. Topic drills, spaced review and weak-area drills in between.
  • Know when you're ready. Once you've answered enough questions, you get an estimate of your chance to pass today, worked out from your own answers, with the topic group to work on next. There's also an exam-day countdown, home-screen widgets for the question of the day and readiness, and an optional daily reminder.
  • A full Morse code course. Koch method lessons, one new character at a time, with Farnsworth spacing and adjustable speed and tone. Clean, click-free tones, and Morse you can feel as vibration, with or without sound. Practice words, call signs, Q-codes and QSOs, copy in your head, and send with an on-screen straight key or iambic paddles.
  • For after you pass. Your privileges by license class, a band chart, Q-codes, prosigns, the phonetic alphabet and the steps to get licensed. Calculators for Ohm's law, decibels, wavelength, antenna length, SWR, reactance and resonance.
  • Built for everyone. Practice exams work fully with TalkBack, and diagrams have text descriptions. The app follows your phone's text size and dark mode.

Free and Pro

Free: Technician study with every question, topic drills and missed-question review; one full practice exam per license class each day; your pass-chance estimate and weakest topic group; Morse lessons 1 to 5, with haptics; all reference tables, calculators, reminders and widgets.

Pro, one purchase: the General and Amateur Extra pools; unlimited practice exams and the full readiness breakdown; spaced review and weak-area drills; the full Morse course from lesson 6, practice sets and sending practice; export and import of your progress.

One-time unlock, no subscription. If Pro isn't on sale in your country yet, the app tells you, and everything free keeps working.

The pools, explained

  • Technician pool 2026–2030: 35 questions, 26 to pass
  • General pool 2023–2027: 35 questions, 26 to pass
  • Amateur Extra pool 2024–2028: 50 questions, 37 to pass

Privacy

Your progress stays on your phone, and in your own Android backup if you use one. No account and no advertising ID. Usage counts and crash reports are optional and can be turned off in Settings. Read the privacy policy.

Sources
