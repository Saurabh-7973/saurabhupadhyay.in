T8C: Operating activities: radio direction finding, contests, linking over the internet, exchanging grid locators

Group T8C of the Technician pool (2026–2030) has 11 active questions. One of them is on every Technician exam. Each is shown word for word; open it to see the correct answer and our explanation.

T8C01

Which of the following methods is used to locate sources of noise interference or jamming?

  1. A Echolocation
  2. B Doppler radar
  3. C Radio direction finding
  4. D Phase locking
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Radio direction finding

Why C is right. C. Radio direction finding is used to locate noise or jamming sources.

Radio direction finding means using a receiver and an antenna that hears best in one direction. You turn the antenna to find where the signal is strongest, then take readings from several places. Where the lines cross is about where the source is.

Why not B? Doppler radar bounces signals off moving objects to measure speed. It does not track down a transmitter.

T8C02

Which of these items would be useful for a hidden transmitter hunt?

  1. A Calibrated SWR meter
  2. B A directional antenna
  3. C A directional wattmeter
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. A directional antenna

Why B is right. B. A directional antenna is useful for a hidden transmitter hunt.

To find a hidden transmitter, you need to know which way the signal is coming from. A directional antenna hears much better in one direction, so pointing it for the strongest signal shows the bearing to the transmitter.

Why not A? An SWR meter checks your own antenna match. It cannot point toward someone else's signal.

T8C03

What operating activity involves contacting as many stations as possible during a specified period?

  1. A Simulated emergency exercises
  2. B Net operations
  3. C Hidden transmitter hunts
  4. D Contesting
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Contesting

Why D is right. D. Contesting means contacting as many stations as you can in a set period.

In a contest, stations try to log as many contacts as possible within the contest hours. Many contests also give extra credit for reaching different areas. Short, accurate exchanges help everyone make more contacts.

Why not B? A net is an organized on-air meeting run by a net control station, not a race for contacts.

T8C04

Which of the following is good practice when contacting another station in a contest?

  1. A Signing only the last two letters of your call if there are many other stations calling
  2. B Contacting the station twice to be sure that you are in his log
  3. C Sending only the minimum information needed for proper identification and the contest exchange
  4. D Adding “Please copy” before your exchange
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Sending only the minimum information needed for proper identification and the contest exchange

Why C is right. C. In a contest, send only the minimum information needed for identification and the exchange.

Contest contacts are quick. Each station sends its call sign and the short exchange the contest rules ask for. Extra words slow things down for you and for everyone waiting. A clean, short exchange is the courteous way to operate.

Why not A? You must give your full call sign. Partial calls make logging harder and do not properly identify your station.

T8C05

What is a grid locator?

  1. A A letter-number designator assigned to a geographic location
  2. B A letter-number designator assigned to an azimuth and elevation
  3. C An instrument for locating faults in power amplifiers
  4. D An instrument for radio direction finding
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. A letter-number designator assigned to a geographic location

Why A is right. A. A grid locator is a letter-number code for a geographic location.

The grid system divides the world into rectangles, each with a short code of letters and numbers. Telling someone your grid square gives your approximate location in a few characters. Hams use grids in contests, awards and VHF and UHF operating.

Why not B? A grid locator names a place on Earth, not an antenna pointing direction.

T8C06

How is over the air access to Internet Radio Linking Project (IRLP) nodes accomplished?

  1. A By obtaining a password that is sent via voice to the node
  2. B By using Dual-Tone Multi-Frequency (DTMF) signals
  3. C By entering the proper internet password
  4. D By using Continuous Tone-Coded Squelch System (CTCSS) tone codes
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. By using Dual-Tone Multi-Frequency (DTMF) signals

Why B is right. B. You reach an IRLP node over the air using DTMF signals.

DTMF tones are the same tones a phone keypad makes. From your radio's keypad, you send a short tone code to the node. The node reads the code and links your local repeater or station to the chosen distant node over the internet.

Why not D? CTCSS tones are low tones that open a repeater's receiver, not codes for picking a node.

T8C07

What is Voice Over Internet Protocol (VoIP)?

  1. A A set of rules specifying how to identify your station when linked over the internet to another station
  2. B A technique employed to “spot” DX stations via the internet
  3. C A technique for measuring the modulation quality of a transmitter using remote sites monitored via the internet
  4. D A method of delivering voice communications over the internet using digital techniques
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. A method of delivering voice communications over the internet using digital techniques

Why D is right. D. VoIP is a way to carry voice over the internet using digital techniques.

Voice Over Internet Protocol turns sound into digital data packets and sends them across the internet. At the other end the packets are turned back into sound. Several ham radio linking systems use VoIP to connect stations and repeaters far apart.

Why not B? Spotting DX stations online is a separate activity and does not carry voice.

T8C08

What is the Internet Radio Linking Project (IRLP)?

  1. A A technique to connect amateur radio systems, such as repeaters, via the internet
  2. B A system for providing access to websites via amateur radio
  3. C A system for informing amateurs in real time of the frequency of active DX stations
  4. D A technique for measuring signal strength of an amateur transmitter via the internet
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. A technique to connect amateur radio systems, such as repeaters, via the internet

Why A is right. A. IRLP connects amateur radio systems, such as repeaters, through the internet.

With IRLP, a repeater or simplex station is connected to a computer called a node. The node uses the internet to link to other nodes. A ham talking on one linked repeater can be heard on a repeater in another city or country.

Why not B? IRLP links radio systems to each other. It does not let you browse websites over the air.

T8C09

Which of the following protocols enables an amateur station to transmit through a repeater without using a radio to initiate the transmission?

  1. A IRLP
  2. B D-STAR
  3. C DMR
  4. D EchoLink
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. EchoLink

Why D is right. D. EchoLink lets you transmit through a repeater without using a radio.

EchoLink can run on a computer or phone. A licensed ham can connect through the internet to an EchoLink node that is linked to a repeater, and their voice goes out over the air from there. No radio is needed at the user's end.

Why not C? DMR is a digital voice mode that you use with a DMR radio.

T8C10

What is required before using the EchoLink system?

  1. A Complete the required EchoLink training
  2. B Purchase a license to use the EchoLink software
  3. C Register your call sign and provide proof of license
  4. D At least a General Class license
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Register your call sign and provide proof of license

Why C is right. C. To use EchoLink, you register your call sign and give proof of license.

EchoLink lets internet users key up real amateur stations. To keep it limited to licensed operators, the system checks each new user. You register your call sign and show that you hold a license before your access is turned on.

Why not D? Technician licensees may use EchoLink. A General license is not required.

T8C11

What is an amateur radio station that connects other amateur stations to the internet?

  1. A A gateway
  2. B A repeater
  3. C A digipeater
  4. D A beacon
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. A gateway

Why A is right. A. A station that connects other amateur stations to the internet is a gateway.

A gateway sits between two kinds of networks. It listens for signals from amateur stations on the air and passes them to the internet, and it sends traffic from the internet back out over the air.

Why not C? A digipeater relays data packets over the air from one radio station to another. It does not need the internet.
