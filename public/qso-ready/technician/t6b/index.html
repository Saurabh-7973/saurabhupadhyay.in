T6B: Semiconductors: basic principles and applications of solid-state devices, diodes and transistors; Gain

Group T6B of the Technician pool (2026–2030) has 12 active questions. One of them is on every Technician exam. Each is shown word for word; open it to see the correct answer and our explanation.

T6B01

Which is true about forward voltage drop in a diode?

  1. A It is lower in some diode types than in others
  2. B It is proportional to peak inverse voltage
  3. C It indicates that the diode is defective
  4. D It has no impact on the voltage delivered to the load
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. It is lower in some diode types than in others

Why A is right. A. It is lower in some diode types than in others.

A diode needs a small voltage across it before it conducts well, and that voltage is lost from the circuit. The amount depends on the material and design. Schottky and germanium diodes drop less than ordinary silicon diodes.

Why not D? The drop subtracts from the voltage, so the load gets a little less.

T6B02

What electronic component allows current to flow in only one direction?

  1. A Resistor
  2. B Fuse
  3. C Diode
  4. D Driven element
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Diode

Why C is right. C. Diode.

A diode lets current pass when its anode is more positive than its cathode and blocks current the other way. That one-way behavior is what lets diodes turn AC into DC.

Why not B? A fuse passes current both ways until it melts open from too much current.

T6B03

Which of these components can be used as an electronic switch?

  1. A Varistor
  2. B Potentiometer
  3. C Transistor
  4. D Thermistor
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Transistor

Why C is right. C. Transistor.

A small current or voltage at a transistor's control lead can turn a much larger current fully on or fully off. With no moving parts, it can switch very fast and many times without wearing out.

Why not D? A thermistor changes resistance with temperature; it has no separate control input.

T6B04

Which of the following components can consist of three regions of semiconductor material?

  1. A Alternator
  2. B Transistor
  3. C Triode
  4. D Pentode
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Transistor

Why B is right. B. Transistor.

A bipolar transistor is a sandwich of three semiconductor layers, either N-P-N or P-N-P. Each region gets its own lead: emitter, base and collector.

Why not C? A triode is a vacuum tube, built from metal parts in a glass envelope, not semiconductor material.

T6B05

What type of transistor has a gate, drain, and source?

  1. A Varistor
  2. B Field-effect
  3. C Hall-effect
  4. D Bipolar junction
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Field-effect

Why B is right. B. Field-effect.

In a field-effect transistor, voltage on the gate creates an electric field that controls current flowing from the source to the drain. The three lead names, gate, drain and source, belong to this type.

Why not D? A bipolar junction transistor's leads are emitter, base and collector.

T6B06

How is the cathode lead of a semiconductor diode often marked on the package?

  1. A With the word "cathode"
  2. B With a stripe
  3. C With the letter C
  4. D With the letter K
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. With a stripe

Why B is right. B. With a stripe.

Most small diodes have a painted band near one end. That band marks the cathode, and it matches the bar at the tip of the triangle in the schematic symbol.

Why not A? Diode packages are usually too small to print a full word.

Remember: Stripe on the part, bar in the symbol: both mark the cathode.

T6B07

What causes a light-emitting diode (LED) to emit light?

  1. A Forward current
  2. B Reverse current
  3. C Capacitively-coupled RF signal
  4. D Inductively-coupled RF signal
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Forward current

Why A is right. A. Forward current.

An LED glows when current flows through it in the forward direction, from anode to cathode. Inside, electrons recombine at the junction and release energy as light. Reversed, it blocks current and stays dark.

Why not B? Reverse current is blocked by the diode, so no light is produced.

T6B08

What does the abbreviation FET stand for?

  1. A Frequency Emission Transmitter
  2. B Fast Electron Transistor
  3. C Free Electron Transmitter
  4. D Field Effect Transistor
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Field Effect Transistor

Why D is right. D. Field Effect Transistor.

FET stands for field effect transistor. The name describes how it works: an electric field from the gate controls the current through the device.

Why not B? There is no common device called a fast electron transistor.

T6B09

What are the names for the electrodes of a diode?

  1. A Plus and minus
  2. B Source and drain
  3. C Anode and cathode
  4. D Gate and base
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Anode and cathode

Why C is right. C. Anode and cathode.

A diode has two electrodes. Current flows in when the anode is positive compared with the cathode. In the symbol, the triangle points from the anode toward the cathode bar.

Why not B? Source and drain are leads of a field-effect transistor.

T6B10

Which of the following can provide power gain?

  1. A Transformer
  2. B Transistor
  3. C Reactor
  4. D Resistor
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Transistor

Why B is right. B. Transistor.

Power gain means the output signal carries more power than the input. A transistor does this by using a small input to control a larger flow of power from the supply.

Why not A? A transformer can raise voltage, but the current drops, so power out never exceeds power in.

T6B11

What does the term gain mean in amplifiers?

  1. A The output signal voltage relative to the input signal voltage
  2. B The output signal current relative to the input signal current
  3. C The output signal power relative to the input signal power
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. All these choices are correct

Why D is right. D. All these choices are correct.

Gain compares an amplifier's output to its input. You can measure that comparison using voltage, current or power, and each is a valid kind of gain. The quantity used depends on what the circuit needs.

T6B12

What are the names of the electrodes of a bipolar junction transistor?

  1. A Signal, bias, power
  2. B Emitter, base, collector
  3. C Input, output, supply
  4. D Pole one, pole two, output
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Emitter, base, collector

Why B is right. B. Emitter, base, collector.

A bipolar junction transistor has three leads. The base is the control lead. A small base current lets a larger current flow between the collector and the emitter.

Why not D? Poles describe switches, not transistors.
