BNS Chapter XIII: Of Contempts Of The Lawful Authority Of Public Servants

Chapter XIII of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 has 21 sections (206–226). All are in force since 1 July 2024. 21 of them are classified in the BNSS First Schedule; the small line under the heading shows the first entry (cognizable, bailable, court).
