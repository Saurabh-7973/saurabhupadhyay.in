BNS Chapter XVIII: Of Offences Relating To Documents And To Property Marks

Chapter XVIII of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 has 16 sections (335–350). All are in force since 1 July 2024. 15 of them are classified in the BNSS First Schedule; the small line under the heading shows the first entry (cognizable, bailable, court).
