G1D: Volunteer Examiners and Volunteer Examiner Coordinators; temporary identification; element credit; remote operation

Group G1D of the General pool (2023–2027) has 12 active questions. One of them is on every General exam. Each is shown word for word; open it to see the correct answer and our explanation.

G1D01

Who may receive partial credit for the elements represented by an expired amateur radio license?

  1. A Any person who can demonstrate that they once held an FCC-issued General, Advanced, or Amateur Extra class license that was not revoked by the FCC
  2. B Anyone who held an FCC-issued amateur radio license that expired not less than 5 and not more than 15 years ago
  3. C Any person who previously held an amateur license issued by another country, but only if that country has a current reciprocal licensing agreement with the FCC
  4. D Only persons who once held an FCC issued Novice, Technician, or Technician Plus license
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Any person who can demonstrate that they once held an FCC-issued General, Advanced, or Amateur Extra class license that was not revoked by the FCC

Why A is right. A. The element credit table gives credit for expired General, Advanced and Extra grants, but not for later Technician grants.

Volunteer Examiners must give exam credit for some licenses even long after they expire. An expired General or Advanced grant earns credit for Element 3, and an expired Extra grant earns Elements 3 and 4. You still pass Element 2, the Technician exam. A Technician license granted on or after March 21, 1987 earns no credit once it is past the grace period.

“(2) Advanced; General; or Technician granted before March 21, 1987 | Elements 2 and 3 | Element 3.”

47 CFR 97.505(a)

Why not D? A modern Technician or Technician Plus license earns no credit once expired past the grace period.

G1D02

What license examinations may you administer as an accredited Volunteer Examiner holding a General class operator license?

  1. A General and Technician
  2. B None, only Amateur Extra class licensees may be accredited
  3. C Technician only
  4. D Amateur Extra, General, and Technician
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Technician only

Why C is right. C. A General class VE meets the license requirement only for giving the Technician exam.

Each Volunteer Examiner (VE) must hold the class the rules set for that exam: General or higher for Technician, Advanced or Extra for General, and Extra for Extra. A General licensee can therefore help give only the Technician exam.

“Amateur Extra, Advanced or General Class in order to administer a Technician Class operator license examination”

47 CFR 97.509(b)(3)(i)

Why not A? Giving the General exam requires an Advanced or Extra license.

G1D03

On which of the following band segments may you operate if you are a Technician class operator and have an unexpired Certificate of Successful Completion of Examination (CSCE) for General class privileges?

  1. A Only the Technician band segments until your upgrade is posted in the FCC database
  2. B Only on the Technician band segments until you have a receipt for the FCC application fee payment
  3. C On any General or Technician class band segment
  4. D On any General or Technician class band segment except 30 meters and 60 meters
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. On any General or Technician class band segment

Why C is right. C. A CSCE showing you passed General lets you use General privileges right away, until the upgrade is processed or 365 days pass.

A Certificate of Successful Completion of Examination (CSCE) is the paper Volunteer Examiners give you after you pass. With it, you may use all the privileges of the new class, on every General and Technician band segment. You do not need to wait for the FCC database or a fee receipt. When you use General privileges before the upgrade appears, add AG after your call sign.

“is authorized to exercise the rights and privileges of the higher operator class until final disposition of the application or until 365 days following the passing of the examination, whichever comes first”

47 CFR 97.9(b)

Why not D? No band is held back; 30 and 60 meters are General privileges like the rest.

G1D04

Who must observe the administration of a Technician class license examination?

  1. A At least three Volunteer Examiners of General class or higher
  2. B At least two Volunteer Examiners of General class or higher
  3. C At least two Volunteer Examiners of Technician class or higher
  4. D At least three Volunteer Examiners of Technician class
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. At least three Volunteer Examiners of General class or higher

Why A is right. A. Every exam session needs a team of at least three VEs, and each must hold General or higher to give a Technician exam.

Part 97 requires at least three Volunteer Examiners (VEs) at every exam. Each VE must watch the examinee for the whole test. For the Technician exam, each VE must hold a General, Advanced or Amateur Extra license.

“Each examination for an amateur operator license must be administered by a team of at least 3 VEs”

47 CFR 97.509(a)

Why not B? Two VEs are too few; the rule requires a team of three.

G1D05

When operating a US station by remote control from outside the country, what license is required of the control operator?

  1. A A US operator/primary station license
  2. B Only an appropriate US operator/primary license and a special remote station permit from the FCC
  3. C Only a license from the foreign country, as long as the call sign includes identification of portable operation in the US
  4. D A license from the foreign country and a special remote station permit from the FCC
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. A US operator/primary station license

Why A is right. A. Part 97 requires a US station's control operator to have a US license grant in the FCC database, and operating remotely from abroad does not change that.

Every amateur station needs a control operator while transmitting. Part 97 says that person must have a license grant in the FCC's database. Running a US station over the internet from abroad does not change this. The FCC has no special remote-operation permit.

“For whom an amateur operator/primary station license grant appears on the ULS consolidated licensee database”

47 CFR 97.7

Why not B? No special remote station permit exists in Part 97.

G1D06

Until an upgrade to General class is shown in the FCC database, when must a Technician licensee identify with “AG” after their call sign?

  1. A Whenever they operate using General class frequency privileges
  2. B Whenever they operate on any amateur frequency
  3. C Whenever they operate using Technician frequency privileges
  4. D A special identifier is not required if their General class license application has been filed with the FCC
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Whenever they operate using General class frequency privileges

Why A is right. A. The AG indicator is needed only while you use General privileges on the strength of your exam certificate.

After passing General, a Technician may use the new privileges before the FCC database shows the upgrade. While doing so, the operator adds AG after the call sign: after a slant mark (/) on CW, or on phone after a word for the slant such as "slash" or "stroke". Many operators say "temporary" as a matter of practice. When operating within Technician privileges, no indicator is needed. Once the upgrade appears in the database, the AG is dropped.

“For a control operator who has requested a license modification from Novice or Technician to General Class: AG”

47 CFR 97.119(f)(2)

Why not B? On Technician frequencies, you already have privileges and need no indicator.

G1D07

Volunteer Examiners are accredited by what organization?

  1. A The Federal Communications Commission
  2. B The Universal Licensing System
  3. C A Volunteer Examiner Coordinator
  4. D The Wireless Telecommunications Bureau
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. A Volunteer Examiner Coordinator

Why C is right. C. Each VE must be accredited by the Volunteer Examiner Coordinator (VEC) that coordinates the exam session.

A Volunteer Examiner Coordinator (VEC) is an organization that works with the FCC to run amateur exams. VECs accredit their own VEs, schedule sessions and send results to the FCC. The FCC itself does not accredit individual VEs.

“Be accredited by the coordinating VEC”

47 CFR 97.509(b)(1)

Why not A? The FCC relies on VECs to accredit examiners; it does not do it directly.

G1D08

Which of the following criteria must be met for a non-US citizen to be an accredited Volunteer Examiner?

  1. A The person must be a resident of the US for a minimum of 5 years
  2. B The person must hold an FCC granted amateur radio license of General class or above
  3. C The person’s home citizenship must be in ITU region 2
  4. D None of these choices is correct; a non-US citizen cannot be a Volunteer Examiner
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. The person must hold an FCC granted amateur radio license of General class or above

Why B is right. B. The VE rules ask for a General or higher FCC license, and say nothing about citizenship.

Part 97 lists what each Volunteer Examiner must have: accreditation by a VEC, age 18 or older, a license of the right class, and no history of a revoked or suspended license. Citizenship and residence are not on the list. So a non-US citizen with an FCC General license or higher can be a VE.

“Be a person who holds an amateur operator license of the class specified below”

47 CFR 97.509(b)(3)

Why not D? No citizenship requirement appears in the VE rules.

G1D09

How long is a Certificate of Successful Completion of Examination (CSCE) valid for exam element credit?

  1. A 30 days
  2. B 180 days
  3. C 365 days
  4. D For as long as your current license is valid
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 365 days

Why C is right. C. A CSCE gives element credit for exams passed within the previous 365 days.

A Certificate of Successful Completion of Examination (CSCE) proves which exam elements you passed. For the next 365 days, it counts as credit toward a license, and it lets you use the new privileges while your upgrade is processed. After a year it no longer counts.

“holds a CSCE indicating that the person has completed the necessary examinations within the previous 365 days”

47 CFR 97.9(b)

Why not D? The CSCE has its own 365-day life, apart from your license term.

G1D10

What is the minimum age that one must be to qualify as an accredited Volunteer Examiner?

  1. A 16 years
  2. B 18 years
  3. C 21 years
  4. D There is no age limit
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 18 years

Why B is right. B. Part 97 sets 18 as the minimum age for a VE.

Volunteer Examiners handle official exam papers and sign forms sent to the FCC, so the rules ask them to be adults. Each VE must be at least 18. Younger hams can still pass exams and hold any license class.

“Be at least 18 years of age”

47 CFR 97.509(b)(2)

Why not D? There is an age limit, and it is 18.

G1D11

What action is required to obtain a new General class license after a previously held license has expired and the two-year grace period has passed?

  1. A They must have a letter from the FCC showing they once held an amateur or commercial license
  2. B There are no requirements other than being able to show a copy of the expired license
  3. C Contact the FCC to have the license reinstated
  4. D The applicant must show proof of the appropriate expired license grant and pass the current Element 2 exam
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. The applicant must show proof of the appropriate expired license grant and pass the current Element 2 exam

Why D is right. D. An expired General grant still earns Element 3 credit, so the applicant shows proof of it and passes only Element 2.

Once the two-year grace period ends, an expired license cannot be renewed. To get back on the air, apply for a new license at an exam session. Show proof of the old General grant; the Volunteer Examiners give credit for Element 3. Then pass the current Element 2 (Technician) exam to earn a new General license.

“(2) Advanced; General; or Technician granted before March 21, 1987 | Elements 2 and 3 | Element 3.”

47 CFR 97.505(a)

Why not C? After the grace period, the FCC does not reinstate the old license.

G1D12

When operating a station in South America by remote control over the internet from the US, what regulations apply?

  1. A Those of both the remote station’s country and the FCC
  2. B Those of the remote station’s country and the FCC’s third-party regulations
  3. C Only those of the remote station’s country
  4. D Only those of the FCC
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Only those of the remote station’s country

Why C is right. C. The radio is in South America, so that country's amateur rules govern its transmissions, not the FCC's.

What matters is where the transmitter is, not where the operator sits. FCC licensing rules apply to stations at places where the FCC regulates the amateur service. A station in another country falls under that country's rules, so you need whatever permission that country requires.

“Within 50 km of the Earth's surface and at a place where the amateur service is regulated by the FCC”

47 CFR 97.5(a)

Why not D? The FCC does not regulate a transmitter located in another country.
