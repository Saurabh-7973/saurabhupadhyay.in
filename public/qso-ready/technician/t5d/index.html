T5D: Ohm’s Law; Series and parallel circuits

Group T5D of the Technician pool (2026–2030) has 14 active questions. One of them is on every Technician exam. Each is shown word for word; open it to see the correct answer and our explanation.

T5D01

What formula is used to calculate current in a circuit?

  1. A I = E x R
  2. B I = E / R
  3. C I = E² x R
  4. D I = E² / R
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. I = E / R

Why B is right. B. I = E / R.

Current grows when you push harder with more voltage and shrinks when resistance is higher. Dividing voltage by resistance captures both effects. Here E is voltage in volts, R is resistance in ohms, and I is current in amperes.

Why not A? Multiplying by R would make current rise as resistance rises, which is backward.

T5D02

What formula is used to calculate voltage in a circuit?

  1. A E = I x R
  2. B E = I / R
  3. C E = I² x R
  4. D E = I² / R
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. E = I x R

Why A is right. A. E = I x R.

Voltage across a resistance equals the current through it times the resistance. More current or more resistance means more voltage. E is in volts, I in amperes, R in ohms.

Why not B? E = I / R would mean more resistance gives less voltage at the same current, which is backward.

T5D03

What formula is used to calculate resistance in a circuit?

  1. A R = E x I
  2. B R = E / I
  3. C R = E + I
  4. D R = E - I
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. R = E / I

Why B is right. B. R = E / I.

Resistance is how many volts it takes to push each ampere through a circuit. So divide voltage by current. If 10 volts drives 2 amperes, resistance is 5 ohms.

Why not A? Multiplying voltage by current gives power, not resistance.

T5D04

What is the resistance of a circuit in which a current of 3 amperes flows when connected to 90 volts?

  1. A 3 ohms
  2. B 30 ohms
  3. C 1/30 ohm
  4. D 270 ohms
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 30 ohms

Why B is right. B. 30 ohms.

Divide the voltage by the current to find resistance.

R = E / I
R = 90 V / 3 A = 30 ohms
= 30 ohms

Why not D? 270 ohms multiplies volts by amperes, which is power, not resistance.

T5D13

In which type of circuit is the current always the same through all components?

  1. A Series
  2. B Parallel
  3. C Resonant
  4. D Branch
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Series

Why A is right. A. Series.

In a series circuit, parts are connected end to end in a single loop. Current has only one path, so the same current passes through every part.

Why not B? In parallel, current splits among branches, so each part can carry a different current.

Remember: One road, so all traffic passes every stop.

T5D14

In which type of circuit is the voltage always the same across all components?

  1. A Series
  2. B Parallel
  3. C Resonant
  4. D Branch
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Parallel

Why B is right. B. Parallel.

In a parallel circuit, every part connects directly across the same two points. Since each part is attached to the same pair of points, each one has the same voltage across it.

Why not A? In series, the voltage divides among the parts, so each can have a different voltage.
