Chapter XIV of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 has 43 sections (227–269). All are in force since 1 July 2024. 41 of them are classified in the BNSS First Schedule; the small line under the heading shows the first entry (cognizable, bailable, court).
