T9B: Feed lines: types, attenuation vs frequency, selecting; SWR concepts; Antenna tuners (couplers); RF Connectors: selecting, weather protection

Group T9B of the Technician pool (2026–2030) has 12 active questions. One of them is on every Technician exam. Each is shown word for word; open it to see the correct answer and our explanation.

T9B01

Which of the following connectors should be carefully taped for weather protection when used outdoors?

  1. A PL259
  2. B BNC
  3. C Type N
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. All these choices are correct

Why D is right. D. All these choices are correct.

PL-259, BNC and Type N connectors can all let water seep in if they are left bare outdoors. Water inside a connector or cable raises loss and causes corrosion. Wrapping outdoor connections tightly with tape or sealant keeps moisture out.

T9B02

What is the most common impedance of coaxial cables used in amateur radio?

  1. A 8 ohms
  2. B 50 ohms
  3. C 600 ohms
  4. D 12 ohms
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 50 ohms

Why B is right. B. 50 ohms is the most common coax impedance in amateur radio.

Most ham transmitters are designed to feed a 50-ohm load. Using coax with the same 50-ohm characteristic impedance keeps the system matched, so power flows to the antenna with little reflected back.

Why not C? 600 ohms is typical of open-wire ladder line, not coax.

T9B03

Why is coaxial cable the most common feed line for amateur radio antenna systems?

  1. A It is easy to use and requires few special installation considerations
  2. B It has less loss than any other type of feed line
  3. C It can handle more power than any other type of feed line
  4. D It is less expensive than any other type of feed line
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. It is easy to use and requires few special installation considerations

Why A is right. A. Coax is popular because it is easy to use and needs few special installation steps.

Coax has its shield wrapped around the center wire, so it keeps the signal inside. You can run it near metal, tape it to a mast, or bury suitable types without much trouble. That convenience is why most hams use it.

Why not B? Coax is not the lowest-loss feed line. Open-wire line and hardline have less loss.

T9B04

What is the major function of an antenna tuner (antenna coupler)?

  1. A It matches the antenna system impedance to the transceiver's output impedance
  2. B It helps a receiver automatically tune in weak stations
  3. C It allows an antenna to be used on both transmit and receive
  4. D It automatically selects the proper antenna for the frequency band being used
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. It matches the antenna system impedance to the transceiver's output impedance

Why A is right. A. An antenna tuner matches the antenna system impedance to the transceiver's output impedance.

A transmitter works best into a load close to its design value, usually 50 ohms. When the antenna system shows a different impedance, an antenna tuner uses coils and capacitors to transform it. The transmitter then sees a good match and delivers power properly.

Why not D? A tuner adjusts the match on one antenna. It does not pick between antennas.

T9B05

What happens as the frequency of a signal in coaxial cable is increased?

  1. A The characteristic impedance decreases
  2. B The loss decreases
  3. C The characteristic impedance increases
  4. D The loss increases
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. The loss increases

Why D is right. D. As frequency increases, loss in coax increases.

Some signal energy is lost as heat in the wire and in the insulation between the conductors. These losses grow as frequency goes up. The same length of cable that is fine on HF can waste a large share of your power on UHF.

Why not A? Characteristic impedance stays about the same as frequency changes. It is the loss that changes.

T9B06

Which of the following connector types is most suitable as an RF connector for frequencies above 400 MHz?

  1. A PL-259
  2. B Type N
  3. C RS-213
  4. D DB-25
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Type N

Why B is right. B. Type N connectors are well suited for frequencies above 400 MHz.

A Type N connector keeps its impedance steady through the joint and seals well. That gives low loss and good performance well into the microwave range. Many hams use it for UHF and higher work.

Why not A? The PL-259 does not keep a steady impedance and works poorly at higher UHF frequencies.

T9B07

Which of the following is true of PL-259 type coax connectors?

  1. A They are preferred for microwave operation
  2. B They are watertight
  3. C They are commonly used at HF and VHF frequencies
  4. D They are a bayonet-type connector
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. They are commonly used at HF and VHF frequencies

Why C is right. C. PL-259 connectors are commonly used at HF and VHF.

The PL-259 is the cable plug that fits the SO-239 socket on many HF radios and VHF mobile radios. It is sturdy and easy to install. Its weak points show up at higher frequencies, so it is mostly used below the upper UHF range.

Why not B? PL-259 connectors are not watertight and must be sealed outdoors.

T9B08

Which of the following is a source of loss in coaxial feed line?

  1. A Water intrusion into coaxial connectors
  2. B High SWR
  3. C Multiple connectors in the line
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. All these choices are correct

Why D is right. D. All these choices are correct.

Water that gets into connectors soaks the cable and raises loss. High SWR means power travels back and forth along the line, losing a bit as heat each trip. Every connector adds a small loss too. All three reduce the power reaching your antenna.

T9B09

What can cause erratic changes in SWR?

  1. A Local thunderstorm
  2. B Loose connection in the antenna or feed line
  3. C Over-modulation
  4. D Overload from a strong local station
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Loose connection in the antenna or feed line

Why B is right. B. A loose connection in the antenna or feed line can cause erratic SWR changes.

A loose joint makes contact some of the time and not at other times. Wind or vibration moves it, so the match keeps changing. That makes an SWR meter jump around. Tightening and checking connectors is a good first step.

Why not C? Over-modulation distorts your signal but does not change the match between line and antenna.

T9B10

What is the electrical difference between RG-58 and RG-213 coaxial cable?

  1. A There is no significant difference between the two types
  2. B RG-58 cable has two shields
  3. C RG-213 cable has less loss at a given frequency
  4. D RG-58 cable can handle higher power levels
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. RG-213 cable has less loss at a given frequency

Why C is right. C. RG-213 has less loss than RG-58 at a given frequency.

RG-213 is a thicker cable with a larger center conductor than RG-58. Bigger conductors waste less energy as heat. So at the same frequency and length, RG-213 delivers more power to the antenna. It can also handle more power.

Why not D? RG-58 is the thinner cable and handles less power than RG-213.

Remember: Thicker cable, less loss.

T9B11

Which of the following types of feed line has the lowest loss?

  1. A 50-ohm flexible coax
  2. B Multi-conductor unbalanced cable
  3. C Air-insulated hardline
  4. D 75-ohm flexible coax
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Air-insulated hardline

Why C is right. C. Air-insulated hardline has the lowest loss of these feed lines.

Hardline uses a solid metal tube as its shield, and air is its main insulation. Air wastes almost no energy, and the large conductors lose little as heat. That combination gives it less loss than flexible coax.

Why not A? Flexible coax uses solid or foam plastic insulation and smaller conductors, which add loss.

T9B12

What is standing wave ratio (SWR)?

  1. A A measure of how well a load is matched to a transmission line
  2. B The ratio of amplifier power output to input
  3. C The transmitter efficiency ratio
  4. D An indication of the quality of your station’s ground connection
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. A measure of how well a load is matched to a transmission line

Why A is right. A. SWR measures how well a load is matched to a transmission line.

SWR stands for standing wave ratio. When the antenna's impedance matches the feed line, power goes out and almost none comes back, giving an SWR near 1:1. A mismatch reflects some power back toward the radio, and the SWR goes up.

Why not B? Comparing amplifier output and input describes gain, not SWR.
