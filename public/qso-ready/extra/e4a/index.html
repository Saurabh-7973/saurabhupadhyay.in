E4A: Test equipment: analog and digital instruments; spectrum analyzers; antenna analyzers; oscilloscopes; RF measurements

Group E4A of the Amateur Extra pool (2024–2028) has 11 active questions. One of them is on every Amateur Extra exam. Each is shown word for word; open it to see the correct answer and our explanation.

E4A01

Which of the following limits the highest frequency signal that can be accurately displayed on a digital oscilloscope?

  1. A Sampling rate of the analog-to-digital converter
  2. B Analog-to-digital converter reference frequency
  3. C Q of the circuit
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Sampling rate of the analog-to-digital converter

Why A is right. A. A digital scope knows the signal only at its sample instants, so the converter's sample rate sets the highest frequency it can show correctly.

The analog-to-digital converter (ADC) measures the input voltage many times per second. To capture a wave it needs more than two samples per cycle, and in practice several more to show the wave's shape. Above that limit the scope cannot draw the signal faithfully.

Why not C? Q describes how sharply a tuned circuit responds; it is not a property of the scope's input.

E4A02

Which of the following parameters does a spectrum analyzer display on the vertical and horizontal axes?

  1. A Signal amplitude and time
  2. B Signal amplitude and frequency
  3. C SWR and frequency
  4. D SWR and time
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Signal amplitude and frequency

Why B is right. B. A spectrum analyzer plots how strong each frequency is, with amplitude up the side and frequency along the bottom.

An oscilloscope shows voltage against time. A spectrum analyzer sweeps across a range of frequencies and draws a peak wherever energy is present. This frequency view lets you see a carrier, its sidebands and any unwanted signals side by side.

Why not A? Amplitude against time is what an oscilloscope shows.

E4A03

Which of the following test instruments is used to display spurious signals and/or intermodulation distortion products generated by an SSB transmitter?

  1. A Differential resolver
  2. B Spectrum analyzer
  3. C Logic analyzer
  4. D Network analyzer
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Spectrum analyzer

Why B is right. B. Spurious signals and intermodulation products each sit on their own frequency, and a spectrum analyzer shows the strength at every frequency at once.

Intermodulation distortion creates new signals at sums and differences of the tones passing through the transmitter. Spurious signals are any unwanted outputs. On a spectrum analyzer each one appears as a separate peak beside the wanted signal, so you can measure how many dB below it each one sits.

Why not C? A logic analyzer records digital ones and zeros over time, not RF energy by frequency.

E4A04

How is compensation of an oscilloscope probe performed?

  1. A A square wave is displayed, and the probe is adjusted until the horizontal portions of the displayed wave are as nearly flat as possible
  2. B A high frequency sine wave is displayed, and the probe is adjusted for maximum amplitude
  3. C A frequency standard is displayed, and the probe is adjusted until the deflection time is accurate
  4. D A DC voltage standard is displayed, and the probe is adjusted until the displayed voltage is accurate
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. A square wave is displayed, and the probe is adjusted until the horizontal portions of the displayed wave are as nearly flat as possible

Why A is right. A. A square wave has sharp edges and flat tops, so any probe mismatch shows up as rounded or spiked flat sections.

A 10:1 probe has a small adjustable capacitor that must balance the scope's input capacitance. Clip the probe to the scope's built-in square-wave test output and turn the trimmer. Under-compensated, the corners round off; over-compensated, they overshoot. When it is right, the top and bottom are flat.

Why not B? One sine wave tests a single frequency, but compensation must be right across a wide range of frequencies at once.

E4A05

What is the purpose of using a prescaler with a frequency counter?

  1. A Amplify low-level signals for more accurate counting
  2. B Multiply a higher frequency signal so a low-frequency counter can display the operating frequency
  3. C Prevent oscillation in a low-frequency counter circuit
  4. D Reduce the signal frequency to within the counter's operating range
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Reduce the signal frequency to within the counter's operating range

Why D is right. D. A prescaler divides the input frequency by a fixed number so it falls inside the range the counter can count.

A counter's logic can only switch so fast. A prescaler is a fast divider, for example by 10 or 100, placed ahead of the counter. The counter measures the lower frequency, and the reading is multiplied back by the same factor.

Why not B? A prescaler divides; multiplying would push the frequency even further beyond the counter's range.

E4A06

What is the effect of aliasing on a digital oscilloscope when displaying a waveform?

  1. A A false, jittery low-frequency version of the waveform is displayed
  2. B The waveform DC offset will be inaccurate
  3. C Calibration of the vertical scale is no longer valid
  4. D Excessive blanking occurs, which prevents display of the waveform
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. A false, jittery low-frequency version of the waveform is displayed

Why A is right. A. When the sample rate is too low for the input, the samples trace out a false, slower wave that seems to drift or jitter.

Aliasing happens when a signal gets fewer than two samples per cycle. The dots the scope collects line up into a lower frequency that is not really there. Because the sample timing is not locked to the real wave, this false wave often looks unstable. A higher sample rate fixes it.

Why not B? Aliasing is a timing problem; it does not shift the DC level.

E4A07

Which of the following is an advantage of using an antenna analyzer compared to an SWR bridge?

  1. A Antenna analyzers automatically tune your antenna for resonance
  2. B Antenna analyzers compute SWR and impedance automatically
  3. C Antenna analyzers display a time-varying representation of the modulation envelope
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Antenna analyzers compute SWR and impedance automatically

Why B is right. B. An antenna analyzer has its own signal source and processor, so it reads out SWR and impedance directly without a transmitter.

An SWR bridge compares forward and reflected power while you transmit, and gives SWR only. An antenna analyzer sends a low-level test signal, measures voltage and current at its port, and calculates SWR, resistance and reactance (the opposition from coils and capacitors) at any frequency you set.

Why not A? An analyzer measures; you still adjust the antenna or tuner yourself.

E4A08

Which of the following is used to measure SWR?

  1. A Directional wattmeter
  2. B Vector network analyzer
  3. C Antenna analyzer
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. All these choices are correct

Why D is right. D. Each of the three instruments can give you SWR, so every choice is correct.

A directional wattmeter reads forward and reflected power, and SWR follows from those two numbers. A vector network analyzer measures the reflection coefficient, the fraction of signal bounced back, which converts directly to SWR. An antenna analyzer calculates SWR from the impedance it measures.

E4A09

Which of the following is good practice when using an oscilloscope probe?

  1. A Minimize the length of the probe's ground connection
  2. B Never use a high-impedance probe to measure a low-impedance circuit
  3. C Never use a DC-coupled probe to measure an AC circuit
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Minimize the length of the probe's ground connection

Why A is right. A. A long ground lead adds inductance that rings with the probe's capacitance and distorts fast edges, so keep it short.

The ground clip wire acts like a small coil. Together with the probe's capacitance it forms a resonant circuit that adds ringing and overshoot to the trace. The loop it makes also picks up stray fields like an antenna. A short ground spring right at the probe tip gives the cleanest view.

Why not C? A DC-coupled probe passes AC fine; it also shows any DC level present.

E4A10

Which trigger mode is most effective when using an oscilloscope to measure a linear power supply’s output ripple?

  1. A Single-shot
  2. B Edge
  3. C Level
  4. D Line
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Line

Why D is right. D. Line trigger starts each sweep in step with the AC mains, which is the same rhythm the ripple follows.

Ripple on a linear supply comes from rectifying the AC line, so in the US it repeats at 60 or 120 Hz. Line trigger uses the mains itself as the timing reference. The trace stands still even when the ripple is tiny and noisy, which would make an edge or level trigger unsteady.

Why not A? Single-shot captures one event, not a steady repeating ripple.

E4A11

Which of the following can be measured with an antenna analyzer?

  1. A Velocity factor
  2. B Cable length
  3. C Resonant frequency of a tuned circuit
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. All these choices are correct

Why D is right. D. An antenna analyzer can measure all three, so every choice is correct.

An analyzer reads impedance across frequency. On an open or shorted length of coax, the frequency where it looks like a short or open gives the cable's length or its velocity factor (how fast signals travel in it compared with light). Loosely coupled to a coil and capacitor, it shows their resonant frequency as a dip.
