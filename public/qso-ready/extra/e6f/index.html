E6F: Electro-optical technology: photoconductivity; photovoltaic devices; optical sensors and encoders; optically isolated switching

Group E6F of the Amateur Extra pool (2024–2028) has 11 active questions. One of them is on every Amateur Extra exam. Each is shown word for word; open it to see the correct answer and our explanation.

E6F01

What absorbs the energy from light falling on a photovoltaic cell?

  1. A Protons
  2. B Photons
  3. C Electrons
  4. D Holes
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Electrons

Why C is right. C. Electrons in the cell absorb the light's energy, gaining enough to break free and flow as current.

Light arrives in tiny packets called photons. When a photon strikes the semiconductor, an electron absorbs its energy and jumps into a state where it can move. The cell's built-in junction pushes these freed electrons one way, producing current.

Why not B? Photons are the light itself; they deliver the energy rather than absorb it.

E6F02

What happens to photoconductive material when light shines on it?

  1. A Resistance decreases
  2. B Resistance increases
  3. C Reflectivity increases
  4. D Reflectivity decreases
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Resistance decreases

Why A is right. A. Light frees extra charge carriers in a photoconductive material, so its resistance drops.

In the dark, few electrons in the material are free to move, so resistance is high. Light gives electrons enough energy to break loose, adding carriers that can carry current. The brighter the light, the lower the resistance. Light-sensing night-light switches work this way.

Why not B? More light means more free carriers, so resistance falls rather than rises.

E6F03

What is the most common configuration of an optoisolator or optocoupler?

  1. A A lens and a photomultiplier
  2. B A frequency-modulated helium-neon laser
  3. C An amplitude-modulated helium-neon laser
  4. D An LED and a phototransistor
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. An LED and a phototransistor

Why D is right. D. An optoisolator usually pairs an LED with a phototransistor sealed in one package.

Current through the LED makes light. The light falls on the phototransistor, which turns on and passes current in a separate circuit. Only light crosses the gap, so the two circuits share no electrical connection.

Why not B? Ordinary optoisolators do not use lasers; a small LED does the job.

E6F04

What is the photovoltaic effect?

  1. A The conversion of voltage to current when exposed to light
  2. B The conversion of light to electrical energy
  3. C The effect that causes a photodiode to emit light when a voltage is applied
  4. D The effect that causes a phototransistor’s beta to decrease when exposed to light
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. The conversion of light to electrical energy

Why B is right. B. The photovoltaic effect is the direct conversion of light into electrical energy.

In a solar cell, light frees electrons near a PN junction. The junction's built-in electric field pushes them in one direction, producing a voltage and current that can power a load. No moving parts or heat engine are involved.

Why not C? Giving off light when a voltage is applied is what an LED does.

E6F05

Which of the following describes an optical shaft encoder?

  1. A A device that detects rotation by interrupting a light source with a patterned wheel
  2. B A device that measures the strength of a beam of light using analog-to-digital conversion
  3. C An optical computing device in which light is coupled between devices by fiber optics
  4. D A device for generating RTTY signals by means of a rotating light source
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. A device that detects rotation by interrupting a light source with a patterned wheel

Why A is right. A. An optical shaft encoder shines light through a patterned wheel on a shaft, and the interruptions show how far and which way it turns.

The wheel has slots or marks. As it turns, a light sensor sees pulses. Counting the pulses gives the amount of rotation, and two offset sensors reveal the direction. Many radio tuning knobs use encoders.

Why not D? Encoders sense rotation; they do not generate RTTY signals.

E6F06

Which of these materials is most commonly used to create photoconductive devices?

  1. A Polyphenol acetate
  2. B Argon
  3. C Crystalline semiconductor
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Crystalline semiconductor

Why C is right. C. Photoconductive devices are most often made from crystalline semiconductors such as silicon or cadmium sulfide.

A semiconductor holds its electrons loosely enough for light to free them. That makes its resistance change with light. Crystalline semiconductors give consistent, predictable behavior.

Why not B? Argon is a gas used in lamps, not a light-sensitive conductor.

E6F07

What is a solid-state relay?

  1. A A relay that uses transistors to drive the relay coil
  2. B A device that uses semiconductors to implement the functions of an electromechanical relay
  3. C A mechanical relay that latches in the on or off state each time it is pulsed
  4. D A semiconductor switch that uses a monostable multivibrator circuit
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. A device that uses semiconductors to implement the functions of an electromechanical relay

Why B is right. B. A solid-state relay uses semiconductors in place of the moving contacts and coil of an electromechanical relay.

In a solid-state relay, a small control signal lights an internal LED, which turns on a transistor, SCR or triac that switches the load. With no moving parts there is no contact wear, no arcing and no clicking.

Why not A? Using transistors to drive a mechanical coil still leaves moving contacts.

E6F08

Why are optoisolators often used in conjunction with solid-state circuits that control 120 VAC circuits?

  1. A Optoisolators provide a low-impedance link between a control circuit and a power circuit
  2. B Optoisolators provide impedance matching between the control circuit and power circuit
  3. C Optoisolators provide an electrical isolation between a control circuit and the circuit being switched
  4. D Optoisolators eliminate the effects of reflected light in the control circuit
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Optoisolators provide an electrical isolation between a control circuit and the circuit being switched

Why C is right. C. Optoisolators let a control circuit switch 120 VAC with only light crossing between them, keeping the two circuits electrically separate.

The low-voltage control side drives an LED. Its light turns on a light-sensitive switch on the AC side. Because no wire joins the two sides, a fault or surge on the AC line cannot reach the control circuit, and people touching the controls are protected from dangerous voltage.

Why not A? The link is high impedance; that isolation is the whole point.

E6F09

What is the efficiency of a photovoltaic cell?

  1. A The output RF power divided by the input DC power
  2. B The output in lumens divided by the input power in watts
  3. C The open-circuit voltage divided by the short-circuit current under full illumination
  4. D The relative fraction of light that is converted to current
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. The relative fraction of light that is converted to current

Why D is right. D. Efficiency is the fraction of the light energy falling on the cell that it turns into electrical energy.

If 1,000 watts of sunlight strike a panel and it delivers 200 watts, its efficiency is 20%. Common silicon panels fall in the high teens to low twenties of percent. The rest of the light is reflected or turned into heat.

Why not C? Voltage divided by current is a resistance, not an efficiency.

E6F10

What is the most common material used in power-generating photovoltaic cells?

  1. A Selenium
  2. B Silicon
  3. C Cadmium sulfide
  4. D Indium arsenide
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Silicon

Why B is right. B. Silicon is the most common material in power-generating solar cells.

Silicon is plentiful, well understood from the chip industry, and has a band gap that suits sunlight. Crystalline silicon panels make up most solar power systems.

Why not A? Selenium was used in early cells and light meters but is far less efficient.

E6F11

What is the approximate open-circuit voltage produced by a fully illuminated silicon photovoltaic cell?

  1. A 0.5 volts
  2. B 0.7 volts
  3. C 1.1 volts
  4. D 1.5 volts
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. 0.5 volts

Why A is right. A. A fully lit silicon solar cell produces about 0.5 volts with no load connected.

The voltage comes from the cell's PN junction, so it is set by silicon's properties, not the cell's size. A bigger cell gives more current but the same voltage. Panels connect many cells in series; 36 cells at about 0.5 V each give enough to charge a 12 V battery.

Why not B? About 0.7 volts is a silicon diode's drop while conducting, not a solar cell's open-circuit voltage.
