BNS Chapter IV: Of Abetment, Criminal Conspiracy And Attempt

Chapter IV of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 has 18 sections (45–62). All are in force since 1 July 2024. 14 of them are classified in the BNSS First Schedule; the small line under the heading shows the first entry (cognizable, bailable, court).

This is an index: headings, the classification and the matching old-law sections. Read the full text of any section in the Gazette (source PDF) or in the app.
