T7A: Station equipment: receivers, transceivers, transmitter amplifiers, RF preamplifiers, transverters; Basic radio circuit concepts and terminology: sensitivity, selectivity, mixers, oscillators, Push-To-Talk (PTT), VFO, modulation

Group T7A of the Technician pool (2026–2030) has 11 active questions. One of them is on every Technician exam. Each is shown word for word; open it to see the correct answer and our explanation.

T7A01

Which term describes the ability of a receiver to detect the presence of a signal?

  1. A RF gain
  2. B Sensitivity
  3. C Selectivity
  4. D Total Harmonic Distortion
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Sensitivity

Why B is right. B. Sensitivity.

Sensitivity is how weak a signal a receiver can still pick up above its own noise. A more sensitive receiver hears fainter stations.

Why not C? Selectivity is about separating nearby signals, not hearing weak ones.

Remember: Sensitivity: hear the faint. Selectivity: separate the crowd.

T7A02

What is a transceiver?

  1. A A device that combines a receiver and transmitter
  2. B A device for matching feed line impedance to 50 ohms
  3. C A device for automatically sending and decoding Morse code
  4. D A device for converting receiver and transmitter frequencies to another band
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. A device that combines a receiver and transmitter

Why A is right. A. A device that combines a receiver and transmitter.

A transceiver puts a transmitter and a receiver in one box, often sharing parts like the frequency control and antenna connection. Most handheld and mobile radios are transceivers.

Why not D? Moving signals to another band is the job of a transverter.

T7A03

Which of the following is used to convert a signal from one frequency to another?

  1. A Phase splitter
  2. B Mixer
  3. C Inverter
  4. D Amplifier
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Mixer

Why B is right. B. Mixer.

A mixer combines two signals and produces new frequencies at their sum and difference. By picking one of those, a radio can move a signal from one frequency to another.

Why not D? An amplifier makes a signal stronger but leaves its frequency the same.

T7A04

Which term describes the ability of a receiver to discriminate between multiple signals?

  1. A Discrimination ratio
  2. B Sensitivity
  3. C Selectivity
  4. D Harmonic distortion
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Selectivity

Why C is right. C. Selectivity.

Selectivity is a receiver's ability to pass the signal you want and reject signals on nearby frequencies. Good filters give good selectivity on a crowded band.

Why not B? Sensitivity is about hearing weak signals, not telling signals apart.

T7A05

What is the name of a circuit that generates a signal at a specific frequency?

  1. A Reactance modulator
  2. B Phase modulator
  3. C Low-pass filter
  4. D Oscillator
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Oscillator

Why D is right. D. Oscillator.

An oscillator is a circuit that feeds part of its output back to its input so it keeps producing a steady signal. A tuned circuit or crystal sets the frequency.

Why not C? A low-pass filter only removes higher frequencies; it creates no signal.

T7A06

What device converts the RF input and output of a transceiver to another band?

  1. A High-pass filter
  2. B Low-pass filter
  3. C Transverter
  4. D Phase converter
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Transverter

Why C is right. C. Transverter.

A transverter takes a transceiver's signal and shifts it to a different band for transmitting, and shifts received signals back. One radio can then work bands it was not built for.

Why not A? A high-pass filter only blocks lower frequencies; it does not shift bands.

T7A07

What is the function of a transceiver’s PTT input?

  1. A Input for a key used to send CW
  2. B Switches transceiver from receive to transmit when grounded
  3. C Provides a transmit tuning tone when grounded
  4. D Input for a preamplifier tuning tone
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Switches transceiver from receive to transmit when grounded

Why B is right. B. Switches transceiver from receive to transmit when grounded.

PTT means push to talk. Pressing the microphone button, or a foot switch or computer interface, connects the PTT line to ground. The radio sees that and goes into transmit.

Why not A? CW keys usually plug into a separate key jack.

T7A08

Which of the following describes combining speech with an RF carrier signal?

  1. A Impedance matching
  2. B Oscillation
  3. C Modulation
  4. D Low-pass filtering
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Modulation

Why C is right. C. Modulation.

Modulation means changing a radio wave, the carrier, so it carries information. For voice, the speech varies the carrier's amplitude, frequency or phase.

Why not B? Oscillation creates the carrier but adds no speech.

T7A09

What is the function of the switch which selects either SSB or CW-FM on some VHF power amplifiers?

  1. A Change the mode of the transmitted signal
  2. B Set the amplifier for proper operation in the selected mode
  3. C Change the frequency range of the amplifier to operate in the proper segment of the band
  4. D Reduce the received signal noise
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Set the amplifier for proper operation in the selected mode

Why B is right. B. Set the amplifier for proper operation in the selected mode.

SSB needs an amplifier that copies the signal's changing level faithfully, called linear operation. FM and CW have a steady level, so the amplifier can run in a more efficient way. The switch picks the right setup for the mode.

Why not A? The mode is set by the transceiver; the amplifier only boosts what it receives.

T7A10

What can be added to the output of a transceiver to increase the transmitted output power?

  1. A A potentiometer
  2. B An RF power amplifier
  3. C An impedance multiplier
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. An RF power amplifier

Why B is right. B. An RF power amplifier.

An RF power amplifier takes the transceiver's output and boosts it to a higher power level before it goes to the antenna.

Why not A? A potentiometer adjusts resistance and cannot add power.

T7A11

What is the function of the Variable Frequency Oscillator (VFO) circuit in a transceiver?

  1. A Set the receive and transmit frequency
  2. B Provide automatic frequency control
  3. C Inject a variable frequency to allow CW reception
  4. D Generate and demodulate single sideband signals
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Set the receive and transmit frequency

Why A is right. A. Set the receive and transmit frequency.

The VFO is the tunable oscillator controlled by the main tuning knob. Turning the knob changes its frequency, which sets where the radio listens and transmits.

Why not B? Automatic frequency control is a separate circuit that corrects drift.
