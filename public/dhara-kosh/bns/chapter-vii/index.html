BNS Chapter VII: Of Offences Against The State

Chapter VII of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 has 12 sections (147–158). All are in force since 1 July 2024. 12 of them are classified in the BNSS First Schedule; the small line under the heading shows the first entry (cognizable, bailable, court).

This is an index: headings, the classification and the matching old-law sections. Read the full text of any section in the Gazette (source PDF) or in the app.
