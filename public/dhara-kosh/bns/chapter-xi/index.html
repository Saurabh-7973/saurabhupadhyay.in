BNS Chapter XI: Of Offences Against The Public Tranquillity

Chapter XI of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 has 9 sections (189–197). All are in force since 1 July 2024. 9 of them are classified in the BNSS First Schedule; the small line under the heading shows the first entry (cognizable, bailable, court).

This is an index: headings, the classification and the matching old-law sections. Read the full text of any section in the Gazette (source PDF) or in the app.
