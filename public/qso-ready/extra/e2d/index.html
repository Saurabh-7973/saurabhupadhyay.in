E2D: Operating methods: digital modes and procedures for VHF and UHF; APRS; EME procedures; meteor scatter procedures

Group E2D of the Amateur Extra pool (2024–2028) has 11 active questions. One of them is on every Amateur Extra exam. Each is shown word for word; open it to see the correct answer and our explanation.

E2D01

Which of the following digital modes is designed for meteor scatter communications?

  1. A WSPR
  2. B MSK144
  3. C Hellschreiber
  4. D APRS
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. MSK144

Why B is right. B. MSK144 is built to send short bursts that fit into the brief reflections from meteor trails.

A meteor leaves an ionized trail that reflects VHF signals for a fraction of a second up to a few seconds. MSK144 uses a fast data rate and repeats its message so the receiver can decode it from one short ping.

Why not A? WSPR is a slow beacon mode for testing paths, not for meteor bursts.

E2D02

What information replaces signal-to-noise ratio when using the FT8 or FT4 modes in a VHF contest?

  1. A RST report
  2. B State abbreviation
  3. C Serial number
  4. D Grid square
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Grid square

Why D is right. D. Many VHF contests count grid squares as multipliers, so the FT8 and FT4 contest mode sends the grid square in place of the signal report.

A grid square is a rectangular area of the Maidenhead locator system, named with letters and digits like FN31.

Why not A? An RST report is not part of the FT8 or FT4 contest exchange.

E2D03

Which of the following digital modes is designed for EME communications?

  1. A MSK144
  2. B PACTOR III
  3. C WSPR
  4. D Q65
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Q65

Why D is right. D. Q65 was designed for weak, fading paths such as moonbounce (EME, Earth-Moon-Earth).

EME signals travel to the moon and back, so they are very weak and smeared by Doppler spread. Q65 uses long, slow symbols and strong error correction, and it can average several tries. That fits the moon path well.

Why not A? MSK144 is the meteor scatter mode.

E2D04

What technology is used for real-time tracking of balloons carrying amateur radio transmitters?

  1. A FT8
  2. B Bandwidth compressed LORAN
  3. C APRS
  4. D PACTOR III
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. APRS

Why C is right. C. APRS is used to track balloons in real time, because each packet carries the balloon's GPS position.

APRS (Automatic Packet Reporting System) sends short position reports. A balloon payload with a GPS receiver and an APRS transmitter beacons its location, and ground stations and internet gateways pass the reports along. Chasers can then follow the balloon on a map.

Why not A? FT8 is a weak-signal contact mode, not a tracking system.

E2D05

What is the characteristic of the JT65 mode?

  1. A Uses only a 65 Hz bandwidth
  2. B Decodes signals with a very low signal-to-noise ratio
  3. C Symbol rate is 65 baud
  4. D Permits fast-scan TV transmissions over narrow bandwidth
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Decodes signals with a very low signal-to-noise ratio

Why B is right. B. JT65 sends long, slow symbols with strong error correction, which lets it decode signals weaker than the noise you hear.

Because each symbol lasts a long time, the receiver can pick it out from noise that would hide a voice or CW signal. That makes it popular for EME and other weak paths.

Why not C? The 65 refers to its 65 tones; its symbol rate is only a few baud.

E2D06

Which of the following is a method for establishing EME contacts?

  1. A Time-synchronous transmissions alternating between stations
  2. B Storing and forwarding digital messages
  3. C Judging optimum transmission times by monitoring beacons reflected from the moon
  4. D High-speed CW identification to avoid fading
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Time-synchronous transmissions alternating between stations

Why A is right. A. Moonbounce signals sit at the noise floor and take about 2.5 seconds for the round trip, so stations agree on clock-timed periods to send and listen.

Both stations agree on a period length and who sends first. Digital moonbounce modes use the same idea, with periods locked to accurate clock time.

Why not C? Beacons do not set the timing; agreed periods based on clock time do.

E2D07

What digital protocol is used by APRS?

  1. A PACTOR
  2. B QAM
  3. C AX.25
  4. D AMTOR
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. AX.25

Why C is right. C. APRS was built on the existing amateur packet system, so its data travels in AX.25 packets.

AX.25 is the amateur version of an older data link protocol. Each packet carries the sender, destination and a path of relays, followed by the data.

Why not A? PACTOR is an HF data mode, not the APRS protocol.

E2D08

What type of packet frame is used to transmit APRS beacon data?

  1. A Acknowledgement
  2. B Burst
  3. C Unnumbered Information
  4. D Connect
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Unnumbered Information

Why C is right. C. APRS beacons use Unnumbered Information (UI) frames, which are sent without setting up a connection.

A UI frame is broadcast to anyone listening. No link is set up, and no acknowledgment is expected. That suits APRS, where one station's position report is useful to many others at once.

Why not D? A connect frame starts a two-way link, which beacons do not use.

E2D09

What type of modulation is used by JT65?

  1. A Multitone AFSK
  2. B PSK
  3. C RTTY
  4. D QAM
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Multitone AFSK

Why A is right. A. JT65 uses multitone audio frequency shift keying, choosing one of 65 tones for each symbol.

Audio FSK (AFSK) means the computer creates audio tones that feed an SSB transmitter. JT65 picks one of many tones per symbol. That makes the signal easy to decode in noise.

Why not B? PSK changes phase, while JT65 changes tone frequency.

E2D10

What does the packet path WIDE3-1 designate?

  1. A Three stations are allowed on frequency, one transmitting at a time
  2. B Three subcarriers are permitted, subcarrier one is being used
  3. C Three digipeater hops are requested with one remaining
  4. D Three internet gateway stations may receive one transmission
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Three digipeater hops are requested with one remaining

Why C is right. C. In a WIDEn-N path the second number counts down at each digipeater, so WIDE3-1 means three hops were asked for and one is left.

A packet that started as WIDE3-3 shows WIDE3-1 after two relays. When the count reaches zero, no more stations relay it.

Why not A? The path controls relaying, not how many stations share the frequency.

E2D11

How do APRS stations relay data?

  1. A By packet ACK/NAK relay
  2. B By C4FM repeaters
  3. C By DMR repeaters
  4. D By packet digipeaters
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. By packet digipeaters

Why D is right. D. APRS packets are short and sent without a connection, so a digipeater can receive each one and send it on again to extend its range.

A digipeater (digital repeater) stores a whole packet, then retransmits it on the same frequency. Internet gateways can also pass packets to online APRS servers.

Why not B? C4FM is a digital voice repeater system, not how APRS relays data.
