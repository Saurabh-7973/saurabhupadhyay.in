G5A: Reactance; inductance; capacitance; impedance; impedance transformation; resonance

Group G5A of the General pool (2023–2027) has 12 active questions. One of them is on every General exam. Each is shown word for word; open it to see the correct answer and our explanation.

G5A01

What happens when inductive and capacitive reactance are equal in a series LC circuit?

  1. A Resonance causes impedance to be very high
  2. B Impedance is equal to the geometric mean of the inductance and capacitance
  3. C Resonance causes impedance to be very low
  4. D Impedance is equal to the arithmetic mean of the inductance and capacitance
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Resonance causes impedance to be very low

Why C is right. C. In a series LC circuit, equal reactances cancel, so impedance falls to a minimum.

Impedance is the total opposition to AC, combining resistance and reactance. In a series circuit, inductive and capacitive reactance push in opposite directions. When they are equal, they cancel, and only the circuit's small resistance is left. That condition is called resonance.

Why not A? Very high impedance at resonance happens in a parallel LC circuit, not a series one.

G5A02

What is reactance?

  1. A Opposition to the flow of direct current caused by resistance
  2. B Opposition to the flow of alternating current caused by capacitance or inductance
  3. C Reinforcement of the flow of direct current caused by resistance
  4. D Reinforcement of the flow of alternating current caused by capacitance or inductance
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Opposition to the flow of alternating current caused by capacitance or inductance

Why B is right. B. Capacitors and inductors store energy and give it back each cycle, and that back-and-forth limits AC without turning energy into heat.

Resistance turns energy into heat. Reactance limits alternating current in a different way. It changes with frequency and is measured in ohms.

Why not A? Opposition caused by resistance is resistance itself, and it affects DC as well.

G5A03

Which of the following is opposition to the flow of alternating current in an inductor?

  1. A Conductance
  2. B Reluctance
  3. C Admittance
  4. D Reactance
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Reactance

Why D is right. D. An inductor opposes AC through its reactance.

An inductor, or coil, resists changes in the current through it. With AC the current is always changing, so the coil pushes back. That opposition is called inductive reactance, and it grows as frequency rises.

Why not B? Reluctance is opposition to magnetic flux in a core, not to current.

G5A04

Which of the following is opposition to the flow of alternating current in a capacitor?

  1. A Conductance
  2. B Reluctance
  3. C Reactance
  4. D Admittance
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Reactance

Why C is right. C. A capacitor opposes AC through its reactance.

A capacitor stores charge on its plates. As AC reverses, the capacitor charges and discharges, which limits the current. That opposition is called capacitive reactance, and it shrinks as frequency rises.

Why not D? Admittance measures how easily current flows, the opposite of opposition.

G5A05

How does an inductor react to AC?

  1. A As the frequency of the applied AC increases, the reactance decreases
  2. B As the amplitude of the applied AC increases, the reactance increases
  3. C As the amplitude of the applied AC increases, the reactance decreases
  4. D As the frequency of the applied AC increases, the reactance increases
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. As the frequency of the applied AC increases, the reactance increases

Why D is right. D. An inductor resists changes in current, and at higher frequency the current changes faster, so it pushes back harder.

The formula is XL = 2 × pi × f × L, where f is frequency and L is inductance. Reactance grows in step with frequency.

Why not B? Inductive reactance depends on frequency and inductance, not on signal amplitude.

G5A06

How does a capacitor react to AC?

  1. A As the frequency of the applied AC increases, the reactance decreases
  2. B As the frequency of the applied AC increases, the reactance increases
  3. C As the amplitude of the applied AC increases, the reactance increases
  4. D As the amplitude of the applied AC increases, the reactance decreases
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. As the frequency of the applied AC increases, the reactance decreases

Why A is right. A. At higher frequency a capacitor charges and discharges more often each second, so more current flows for the same voltage, which means less opposition.

The formula is XC = 1 / (2 × pi × f × C), where f is frequency and C is capacitance.

Why not B? Rising reactance with frequency describes an inductor, not a capacitor.

G5A07

What is the term for the inverse of impedance?

  1. A Conductance
  2. B Susceptance
  3. C Reluctance
  4. D Admittance
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Admittance

Why D is right. D. Admittance is the inverse of impedance.

Impedance tells how much a circuit opposes AC. Admittance flips that around and tells how easily AC flows. You find it by dividing 1 by the impedance. It is measured in siemens.

Why not A? Conductance is the inverse of resistance alone, not of the full impedance.

G5A08

What is impedance?

  1. A The ratio of current to voltage
  2. B The product of current and voltage
  3. C The ratio of voltage to current
  4. D The product of current and reactance
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. The ratio of voltage to current

Why C is right. C. Impedance extends Ohm's law to AC: it is the total opposition from resistance and reactance, found by dividing voltage by current.

It is measured in ohms, like resistance. For a plain resistor, impedance and resistance are the same.

Why not A? Current divided by voltage gives admittance, the inverse of impedance.

G5A09

What unit is used to measure reactance?

  1. A Farad
  2. B Ohm
  3. C Ampere
  4. D Siemens
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Ohm

Why B is right. B. Reactance is measured in ohms.

Reactance limits current the way resistance does, so it uses the same unit. A capacitor with 50 ohms of reactance at some frequency opposes AC about as much as a 50-ohm resistor, though it stores energy instead of turning it into heat.

Why not D? The siemens measures conductance and admittance, which are inverses of opposition.

G5A10

Which of the following devices can be used for impedance matching at radio frequencies?

  1. A A transformer
  2. B A Pi-network
  3. C A length of transmission line
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. All these choices are correct

Why D is right. D. All three can match impedances at radio frequencies.

A transformer changes impedance by the square of its turns ratio. A pi-network is a capacitor, a coil and another capacitor arranged like the Greek letter pi, used in amplifiers and tuners. A section of transmission line of the right length can also transform one impedance into another.

G5A11

What letter is used to represent reactance?

  1. A Z
  2. B X
  3. C B
  4. D Y
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. X

Why B is right. B. X stands for reactance.

Engineers write reactance as X, with XL for an inductor and XC for a capacitor. Other letters are already taken: Z is impedance, B is susceptance and Y is admittance.

Why not A? Z stands for impedance, not reactance.

G5A12

What occurs in an LC circuit at resonance?

  1. A Current and voltage are equal
  2. B Resistance is cancelled
  3. C The circuit radiates all its energy in the form of radio waves
  4. D Inductive reactance and capacitive reactance cancel
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Inductive reactance and capacitive reactance cancel

Why D is right. D. An inductor's reactance rises with frequency while a capacitor's falls, and since they act in opposite directions, at one frequency they are equal and wipe each other out.

That frequency is the circuit's resonant frequency.

Why not B? Resistance stays in the circuit at resonance; only the reactances cancel.
