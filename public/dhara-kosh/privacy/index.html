Dhara Kosh is a reference app for India's criminal laws (BNS, BNSS, BSA and the old IPC, CrPC and Evidence Act). This policy explains, in plain words, what the app keeps on your phone, what leaves your phone, who receives it, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, phone number or email inside the app.
  • Your searches, bookmarks, notes and drill answers stay on your phone. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • There are no ads and no advertising ID.
  • The app asks for no location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or photo permissions.
  • If you buy the one-time unlock, RevenueCat (our purchase service) keeps a record of the purchase so you can restore it.
  • If you leave "Share anonymous usage" on, the app sends anonymous usage counts to Google Firebase Analytics. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.
  • If the app crashes, a crash report goes to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the bug.
  • If you email us from the app, you choose what to send.

What stays on your phone

These are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see them.

  • Everything you type into search, and your recent searches
  • Bookmarks, highlights, notes and collections
  • Drill and practice answers, scores and review schedules
  • Your settings (language, theme, text size, usage sharing)
  • Whether you have bought the unlock (a cached copy, so the app works offline)
  • The library of Acts itself

What leaves your phone, and who receives it

1. RevenueCat (purchases)

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to handle the one-time unlock through Google Play. The app contacts RevenueCat when it starts (to check whether you have the unlock), when you open the unlock screen (to show the price), and when you buy or restore.

What is sent:

  • a random ID that RevenueCat creates for this install of the app (it is not your name, email or Google account)
  • your Google Play purchase details: product (criminallaws_pro_lifetime), order ID, purchase token, purchase time, price, currency and store country
  • technical details needed to process the request: app version, RevenueCat SDK version, Android version, device language, and your IP address as seen by RevenueCat's servers

Why: to confirm the purchase with Google, unlock the paid features, and let you restore them on a new phone. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

2. Google Play (payment and ratings)

Payments happen inside Google Play. Your card, UPI or bank details go to Google, never to us or to the app. If the app shows Google Play's rating dialog, your rating goes to Google Play under your Google account; the app does not learn whether or how you rated. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Google Firebase Analytics (anonymous usage)

Only from the released app, and only while Settings → "Share anonymous usage" is on (it is on by default; turning it off stops all usage events straight away).

What is sent:

  • that the app was opened
  • that a search was made, with the number of characters typed, the kind of search (section number, Act and section, words, or Hindi) and the number of results. Never the words you searched.
  • that a section was opened: only which Act it belongs to (BNS, BNSS or BSA), how it was reached, and a running count of how many sections this install has opened. Never the section number: which offence someone reads can say something about their situation
  • that the converter was used, with only the Act (for example "IPC"), never the section number
  • that the unlock screen was shown and from where; that a purchase completed, failed (with "cancelled" or "failed") or was restored
  • for a finished practice session: its kind, number of questions, score percentage and duration
  • that "Report an error" was opened, with only the Act; a change of app language; a content update version number
  • what Firebase collects automatically: a random app-instance ID, app version, Android version, device brand and model, device language, approximate country or region worked out from the IP address, how the app was found on Google Play (the Play install referrer), session start and engagement time

What is never sent: your search words, notes, highlights, bookmarks, drill answers, name, email, phone number, Google account, or advertising ID.

Why: to see which features are used, whether search finds what people look for, and whether the app is worth improving. Usage data is kept for 2 months and then deleted automatically. Firebase's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

Only from the released app. When the app crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a crash report: the technical error and where in the code it happened, app version, Android version, device manufacturer and model, free memory and storage, screen orientation, a random installation ID and the time. If usage sharing is on, the report also lists the last few usage events from section 3. Crash reports never contain your search words or notes.

Why: to find and fix crashes. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

5. Emails you choose to send

"Report an error" and "Feedback" open your own email app with a draft addressed to saurabhupadhyay7973@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you press Send there. The draft starts with the app version, content version, Android version and phone model (and, for an error report, the section), so we can reproduce the problem. You can edit or delete any of it before sending. When you send, we receive your email address and whatever the email contains, and we use it only to reply and fix the issue.

5a. Copy and Share

When you tap Copy or Share on a section, the app puts the section text (with its citation, source and disclaimers) on your clipboard, or opens Android's share sheet with that text or with an image card of the same section (also carrying the citation, source and disclaimers). It goes only where you choose to send it, and the app you choose handles it under its own policy.

5b. Read aloud

"Read aloud" hands the section text to your phone's own text-to-speech engine (for example Google's Speech Services). The app sends it nowhere else; the engine works under its own policy, and some engines may use the internet for some voices.

6. Links you open

Source links (for example to egazette.gov.in) open in your browser. That website may log your visit under its own policy. The app sends nothing with the link.

7. Library updates

From time to time the app checks for a corrections update to the library of Acts and downloads it from a public file server (jsDelivr or GitHub). No personal data or ID is sent; like any download, the server sees your IP address.

What we do not do

  • No ads, and no advertising ID.
  • No selling or renting of data, and no sharing with data brokers.
  • RevenueCat and Google receive data only to provide their services to us, as described above.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or photo access.

Children

The app is meant for adults (18+): law students, exam aspirants and professionals. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

All data the app sends travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data on your phone is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Deleting your data

1. Delete what is on your phone (in the app).
Open the app → Me → Settings → Delete my data → confirm. This erases your bookmarks, highlights, notes, collections, recent searches, practice history and settings from the phone, and resets settings to their defaults (except that usage sharing stays off if you had switched it off), and switches off the daily reminder. It does not cancel your unlock: open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchases to get it back. You can clear only recent searches with Settings → Clear recent searches. To keep a copy first, use Settings → Export my data: it makes one file with everything listed above, which you can save or send through your phone's share menu. The app does not upload it anywhere.

2. Uninstall the app.
Uninstalling removes everything the app stored on your phone, including the random RevenueCat and Firebase IDs for this install.

3. Ask us to delete data held by RevenueCat or Firebase.
Email saurabhupadhyay7973@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".

  • For your purchase record at RevenueCat, include the Google Play order number from your purchase receipt email (it starts with "GPA."). We will delete the RevenueCat customer record linked to it within 30 days and confirm by email. After that, Restore may not find the purchase in the app until you buy or restore again through Google Play; Google keeps its own purchase record under your Google account.
  • Usage data in Firebase Analytics and crash reports in Crashlytics are not linked to your name or email, so we cannot look them up by those. Turn off "Share anonymous usage" to stop new usage data; existing usage data is deleted automatically after 2 months and crash reports after 90 days. If you can give us details that let us find a specific crash report (date, time and phone model), we will delete it on request.
  • To stop sending anything at all, uninstall the app.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and raise a grievance. Write to the contact email above; we reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

किसी धारा पर कॉपी या शेयर दबाने पर ऐप धारा का पाठ (संदर्भ, स्रोत और अस्वीकरण के साथ) क्लिपबोर्ड पर रखता है, या Android की शेयर सूची खोलता है, उसी पाठ के साथ या उसी धारा के चित्र-कार्ड के साथ (उसमें भी संदर्भ, स्रोत और अस्वीकरण होते हैं)। यह सिर्फ़ वहीं जाता है जहाँ आप भेजना चुनते हैं, और चुना गया ऐप उसे अपनी नीति के अनुसार संभालता है।

5b. पढ़कर सुनाना

"पढ़कर सुनाएँ" धारा का पाठ आपके फ़ोन के अपने टेक्स्ट-टू-स्पीच इंजन (जैसे Google की Speech Services) को देता है। ऐप इसे और कहीं नहीं भेजता; इंजन अपनी नीति के अनुसार काम करता है, और कुछ इंजन कुछ आवाज़ों के लिए इंटरनेट का इस्तेमाल कर सकते हैं।

6. आपके खोले लिंक

स्रोत के लिंक (जैसे egazette.gov.in) आपके ब्राउज़र में खुलते हैं। वह वेबसाइट अपनी नीति के अनुसार आपकी विज़िट दर्ज कर सकती है। ऐप लिंक के साथ कुछ नहीं भेजता।

7. लाइब्रेरी के अपडेट

ऐप समय-समय पर अधिनियमों की लाइब्रेरी के सुधार-अपडेट देखता है और उन्हें एक सार्वजनिक फ़ाइल सर्वर (jsDelivr या GitHub) से डाउनलोड करता है। कोई निजी जानकारी या ID नहीं भेजी जाती; किसी भी डाउनलोड की तरह, सर्वर को आपका IP पता दिखता है।

हम क्या नहीं करते

  • कोई विज्ञापन नहीं, और कोई विज्ञापन ID नहीं।
  • जानकारी बेचना या किराए पर देना नहीं, और डेटा ब्रोकर से साझा करना नहीं।
  • RevenueCat और Google को जानकारी सिर्फ़ ऊपर बताई सेवाएँ देने के लिए मिलती है।
  • लोकेशन, संपर्क, कैमरा, माइक्रोफ़ोन, स्टोरेज या फ़ोटो तक कोई पहुँच नहीं।

सुरक्षा

ऐप जो भी जानकारी भेजता है, वह एन्क्रिप्टेड कनेक्शन (HTTPS) से जाती है। फ़ोन पर जानकारी ऐप के निजी स्टोरेज में रहती है, जिसे दूसरे ऐप नहीं पढ़ सकते।

अपनी जानकारी मिटाना (Deleting your data)

1. फ़ोन पर रखी जानकारी मिटाएँ (ऐप में)।
