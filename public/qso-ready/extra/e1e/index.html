E1E: Volunteer examiner program: definitions; qualifications; preparation and administration of exams; reimbursement; accreditation; question pools; documentation requirements

Group E1E of the Amateur Extra pool (2024–2028) has 11 active questions. One of them is on every Amateur Extra exam. Each is shown word for word; open it to see the correct answer and our explanation.

E1E01

For which types of out-of-pocket expenses do the Part 97 rules state that VEs and VECs may be reimbursed?

  1. A Preparing, processing, administering, and coordinating an examination for an amateur radio operator license
  2. B Teaching an amateur operator license examination preparation course
  3. C No expenses are authorized for reimbursement
  4. D Providing amateur operator license examination preparation training materials
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Preparing, processing, administering, and coordinating an examination for an amateur radio operator license

Why A is right. A. VEs serve without pay, so the rule lets examinees cover only the real costs of running the exam itself.

Sessions cost money for printing, mailing and room rent. Teaching classes or selling study materials is not part of this allowance.

“VEs and VECs may be reimbursed by examinees for out-of-pocket expenses incurred in preparing, processing, administering, or coordinating an examination”

47 CFR 97.527

Why not B? Teaching a class is not one of the listed expense types.

E1E02

Who is tasked by Part 97 with maintaining the pools of questions for all US amateur license examinations?

  1. A The VEs
  2. B The FCC
  3. C The VECs
  4. D The ARRL
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. The VECs

Why C is right. C. The VECs must work together to keep up one question pool for each exam element.

Each pool must have at least 10 times as many questions as an exam uses, and it must be public before it is used. The FCC does not write the questions.

“All VECs must cooperate in maintaining one question pool for each written examination element.”

47 CFR 97.523

Why not B? The rule puts this duty on the VECs, not the FCC.

E1E03

What is a Volunteer Examiner Coordinator?

  1. A A person who has volunteered to administer amateur operator license examinations
  2. B An organization paid by the volunteer examiner team to publicize and schedule examinations
  3. C An organization that has entered into an agreement with the FCC to coordinate, prepare, and administer amateur operator license examinations
  4. D The person who has entered into an agreement with the FCC to be the VE session manager
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. An organization that has entered into an agreement with the FCC to coordinate, prepare, and administer amateur operator license examinations

Why C is right. C. The rule bars any organization from acting as a VEC unless it has a written agreement with the FCC.

Volunteer Examiner Coordinators accredit VEs, coordinate sessions and send results to the FCC. The VEs themselves give the exams.

“No organization may serve as a VEC unless it has entered into a written agreement with the FCC.”

47 CFR 97.521

Why not A? A person who gives exams is a VE, not a VEC.

E1E04

What is required to be accredited as a Volunteer Examiner?

  1. A Each General, Advanced and Amateur Extra class operator is automatically accredited as a VE when the license is granted
  2. B The amateur operator applying must pass a VE examination administered by the FCC Enforcement Bureau
  3. C The prospective VE must obtain accreditation from the FCC
  4. D A VEC must confirm that the VE applicant meets FCC requirements to serve as an examiner
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. A VEC must confirm that the VE applicant meets FCC requirements to serve as an examiner

Why D is right. D. Accreditation comes from the VEC, and the rule forbids a VEC to accredit anyone who fails the minimum qualifications.

A VEC also turns down people it finds not competent or whose honesty is in question. Holding a certain license class does not by itself make you a VE.

“No VEC may accredit a person as a VE if: (1) The person does not meet minimum VE statutory qualifications or minimum qualifications as prescribed by this part”

47 CFR 97.525(a)

Why not C? Accreditation comes from a VEC, not from the FCC.

E1E05

What must the VE team do with the application form if the examinee does not pass the exam?

  1. A Maintain the application form with the VEC’s records
  2. B Return the application document to the examinee
  3. C Send the application form to the FCC and inform the FCC of the grade
  4. D Destroy the application form
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Return the application document to the examinee

Why B is right. B. A failed element leads to no license grant, so the rule sends the form back to the examinee along with the grade.

The application form does not go to the VEC or FCC. The examinee can try again at another session.

“the administering VEs must return the application document to the examinee and inform the examinee of the grade”

47 CFR 97.509(j)

Why not C? Failed exams are not reported to the FCC.

E1E06

Who is responsible for the proper conduct and necessary supervision during an amateur operator license examination session?

  1. A The VEC coordinating the session
  2. B The designated monitoring VE
  3. C Each administering VE
  4. D Only the VE session manager
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Each administering VE

Why C is right. C. The rule places the duty for proper conduct and supervision on the administering VEs as a group, not on one leader.

Each administering VE must also watch the examinee for the whole exam.

“The administering VEs are responsible for the proper conduct and necessary supervision of each examination.”

47 CFR 97.509(c)

Why not D? The rule does not place this duty on one session manager.

E1E07

What should a VE do if a candidate fails to comply with the examiner’s instructions during an amateur operator license examination?

  1. A Warn the candidate that continued failure to comply will result in termination of the examination
  2. B Immediately terminate the candidate’s examination
  3. C Allow the candidate to complete the examination, but invalidate the results
  4. D Immediately terminate everyone’s examination and close the session
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Immediately terminate the candidate’s examination

Why B is right. B. The rule leaves no room for a warning: failure to follow instructions requires immediate termination of that exam.

Examinees must follow VE instructions. Only that examinee's test ends; others continue.

“The administering VEs must immediately terminate the examination upon failure of the examinee to comply with their instructions.”

47 CFR 97.509(c)

Why not A? The rule calls for immediate termination, not a warning.

E1E08

To which of the following examinees may a VE not administer an examination?

  1. A Employees of the VE
  2. B Friends of the VE
  3. C Relatives of the VE as listed in the FCC rules
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. Relatives of the VE as listed in the FCC rules

Why C is right. C. To prevent conflicts of interest, the rule lists family members a VE may not examine.

The list includes a spouse, children, parents, siblings, grandparents, aunts, uncles, nieces, nephews, in-laws and step relatives. Friends and employees are not on it.

“No VE may administer an examination to his or her spouse, children, grandchildren, stepchildren, parents”

47 CFR 97.509(d)

Why not A? Employees are not among the listed relatives.

E1E09

What may be the penalty for a VE who fraudulently administers or certifies an examination?

  1. A Revocation of the VE’s amateur station license grant and the suspension of the VE’s amateur operator license grant
  2. B A fine of up to $1,000 per occurrence
  3. C A sentence of up to one year in prison
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Revocation of the VE’s amateur station license grant and the suspension of the VE’s amateur operator license grant

Why A is right. A. The rule ties exam fraud directly to the VE's own licenses: station license revocation and operator license suspension.

The rule bars giving or certifying exams by fraud, or for pay beyond allowed expenses.

“Violation of this provision may result in the revocation of the grant of the VE's amateur station license and the suspension of the grant of the VE's amateur operator license.”

47 CFR 97.509(e)

Why not B? The rule names license penalties, not a specific dollar fine.

E1E10

What must the administering VEs do after the administration of a successful examination for an amateur operator license?

  1. A They must collect and send the documents directly to the FCC
  2. B They must collect and submit the documents to the coordinating VEC for grading
  3. C They must submit the application document to the coordinating VEC according to the coordinating VEC instructions
  4. D They must return the documents to the applicant for submission to the FCC according to the FCC instructions
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. They must submit the application document to the coordinating VEC according to the coordinating VEC instructions

Why C is right. C. The VEC is the go-between with the FCC, so the rule has VEs send the application to it in the way it directs.

The VEC checks the paperwork and files it with the FCC. Grading is done by the VEs at the session.

“the administering VEs must submit the application document to the coordinating VEC according to the coordinating VEC's instructions”

47 CFR 97.509(m)

Why not B? The VEs grade the exam; the VEC does not.

E1E11

What must the VE team do if an examinee scores a passing grade on all examination elements needed for an upgrade or new license?

  1. A Photocopy all examination documents and forward them to the FCC for processing
  2. B Three VEs must certify that the examinee is qualified for the license grant and that they have complied with the administering VE requirements
  3. C Issue the examinee the new or upgrade license
  4. D All these choices are correct
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. Three VEs must certify that the examinee is qualified for the license grant and that they have complied with the administering VE requirements

Why B is right. B. A license grant rests on the VEs' word, so the rule requires 3 VEs to certify both the result and that the exam was run properly.

The certifying VEs are each accountable for the exam they report. The FCC grants the license; VEs never issue it.

“3 VEs must certify that the examinee is qualified for the license grant and that the VEs have complied with these administering VE requirements”

47 CFR 97.509(i)

Why not C? The FCC grants licenses; VEs cannot issue them.
