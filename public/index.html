Guidester

In-app tester feedback for Flutter. Testers tap the spot that's wrong, type what's wrong, and it lands on your dashboard with a screenshot, the screen name, the device, the build and the error that caused it.

It doesn't take over your app

No pushed route, no swapped widget tree. It wraps your MaterialApp builder and stays out of your navigator.

Screen names without a router dependency

Four fallback layers resolve the screen name, so it works whether or not you use go_router, auto_route or beamer.

Self-hosted by design

Screenshots go to your own Supabase project. There is no Guidester server in between, and nothing to subscribe to.

The tester hears back

Mark a report fixed and the tester is asked on their next launch whether it actually works now.
