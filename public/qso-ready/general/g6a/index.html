G6A: Resistors; capacitors; inductors; rectifiers; solid-state diodes and transistors; vacuum tubes; batteries

Group G6A of the General pool (2023–2027) has 12 active questions. One of them is on every General exam. Each is shown word for word; open it to see the correct answer and our explanation.

G6A01

What is the minimum allowable discharge voltage for maximum life of a standard 12-volt lead-acid battery?

  1. A 6 volts
  2. B 8.5 volts
  3. C 10.5 volts
  4. D 12 volts
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 10.5 volts

Why C is right. C. Stop discharging a 12-volt lead-acid battery at about 10.5 volts.

A 12-volt lead-acid battery has six cells in series. Each cell is near empty at about 1.75 volts, and 6 × 1.75 is 10.5 volts. Discharging further damages the plates inside and shortens the battery's life.

Why not A? 6 volts is a deep discharge that would badly harm the battery.

G6A02

What is an advantage of batteries with low internal resistance?

  1. A Long life
  2. B High discharge current
  3. C High voltage
  4. D Rapid recharge
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. High discharge current

Why B is right. B. Low internal resistance lets a battery deliver a high current.

Every battery has some resistance inside it. When current flows, that resistance wastes power and drops the voltage at the terminals. With low internal resistance, the battery can supply a large current, like a transmitter needs, without its voltage sagging much.

Why not C? A battery's voltage is set by its chemistry and number of cells, not its internal resistance.

G6A03

What is the approximate forward threshold voltage of a germanium diode?

  1. A 0.1 volt
  2. B 0.3 volts
  3. C 0.7 volts
  4. D 1.0 volts
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. 0.3 volts

Why B is right. B. A germanium diode starts conducting at about 0.3 volts.

A diode lets current flow one way once the forward voltage passes a threshold. That threshold depends on the material. Germanium has a lower threshold than silicon, which makes it useful for detecting weak signals.

Why not C? About 0.7 volts is the threshold for silicon, not germanium.

G6A04

Which of the following is characteristic of an electrolytic capacitor?

  1. A Tight tolerance
  2. B Much less leakage than any other type
  3. C High capacitance for a given volume
  4. D Inexpensive RF capacitor
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. High capacitance for a given volume

Why C is right. C. An extremely thin oxide layer serves as the insulator on a large, rolled-up foil, and both raise the capacitance that fits in a small can.

The tradeoffs are wide tolerance, some leakage current, and the need to connect it with the right polarity.

Why not B? Electrolytic capacitors leak more than most other types, not less.

G6A05

What is the approximate forward threshold voltage of a silicon junction diode?

  1. A 0.1 volt
  2. B 0.3 volts
  3. C 0.7 volts
  4. D 1.0 volts
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. 0.7 volts

Why C is right. C. A silicon junction diode starts conducting at about 0.7 volts.

A diode conducts in the forward direction once the voltage passes a threshold set by its material. For silicon, that is about 0.7 volts. Below that, very little current flows.

Why not B? About 0.3 volts is the threshold for germanium, not silicon.

G6A06

Why should wire-wound resistors not be used in RF circuits?

  1. A The resistor’s tolerance value would not be adequate
  2. B The resistor’s inductance could make circuit performance unpredictable
  3. C The resistor could overheat
  4. D The resistor’s internal capacitance would detune the circuit
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. The resistor’s inductance could make circuit performance unpredictable

Why B is right. B. The resistance wire is wound as a coil, and at RF that coil's inductance adds reactance that changes with frequency.

So at radio frequencies the part no longer acts like a plain resistor. Use carbon or metal film resistors in RF circuits.

Why not D? Capacitance between turns is small; the coil's inductance is the bigger effect.

G6A07

What are the operating points for a bipolar transistor used as a switch?

  1. A Saturation and cutoff
  2. B The active region (between cutoff and saturation)
  3. C Peak and valley current points
  4. D Enhancement and depletion modes
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Saturation and cutoff

Why A is right. A. A transistor switch works at saturation and cutoff.

At cutoff, the transistor is fully off and almost no current flows. At saturation, it is fully on and conducts as much as the circuit allows, with only a small voltage across it. Switching between these two states wastes little power.

Why not B? The active region is used for amplifiers, where the output follows the input smoothly.

G6A08

Which of the following is characteristic of low voltage ceramic capacitors?

  1. A Tight tolerance
  2. B High stability
  3. C High capacitance for given volume
  4. D Comparatively low cost
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: D. Comparatively low cost

Why D is right. D. Low-voltage ceramic capacitors are relatively inexpensive.

Ceramic capacitors are small, cheap and common in many circuits. Many types of low-voltage ceramics drift with temperature and have loose tolerances, so cost is their main selling point.

Why not C? High capacitance for the size is the strength of electrolytic capacitors.

G6A09

Which of the following describes MOSFET construction?

  1. A The gate is formed by a back-biased junction
  2. B The gate is separated from the channel by a thin insulating layer
  3. C The source is separated from the drain by a thin insulating layer
  4. D The source is formed by depositing metal on silicon
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: B. The gate is separated from the channel by a thin insulating layer

Why B is right. B. MOSFET means metal-oxide-semiconductor, which describes the build: a gate on top of an oxide insulator above the channel.

The gate's voltage controls the channel through its electric field, so almost no gate current flows.

Why not A? A gate formed by a reverse-biased junction describes a JFET, a different kind of transistor.

G6A10

Which element of a vacuum tube regulates the flow of electrons between cathode and plate?

  1. A Control grid
  2. B Suppressor grid
  3. C Screen grid
  4. D Trigger electrode
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. Control grid

Why A is right. A. The control grid regulates the electron flow from cathode to plate.

In a vacuum tube, electrons leave the hot cathode and travel to the plate. The control grid is a wire mesh between them. Making the grid more negative pushes electrons back and reduces the flow, so a small grid voltage controls a large plate current.

Why not C? The screen grid's main job is to shield the control grid from the plate.

G6A11

What happens when an inductor is operated above its self-resonant frequency?

  1. A Its reactance increases
  2. B Harmonics are generated
  3. C It becomes capacitive
  4. D Catastrophic failure is likely
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: C. It becomes capacitive

Why C is right. C. Above its self-resonant frequency, an inductor acts like a capacitor.

A real inductor has small capacitance between its turns. At one frequency, called the self-resonant frequency, that capacitance resonates with the inductance. Above that frequency, the capacitance wins out, and the part behaves like a capacitor.

Why not A? Above self-resonance the part acts capacitive, so its reactance falls with frequency instead of rising.

G6A12

What is the primary purpose of a screen grid in a vacuum tube?

  1. A To reduce grid-to-plate capacitance
  2. B To increase efficiency
  3. C To increase the control grid resistance
  4. D To decrease plate resistance
Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: A. To reduce grid-to-plate capacitance

Why A is right. A. An RF-grounded screen between the control grid and the plate shields one from the other, cutting the feedback that can make an amplifier oscillate.

The screen grid is held at a steady DC voltage and connected to ground for RF. With less capacitance between grid and plate, less of the output leaks back to the input.

Why not B? The screen grid's main purpose is shielding, not efficiency.
